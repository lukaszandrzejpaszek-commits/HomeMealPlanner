❓ How it Works

When you click "Load Sample Meals", 12 diverse meals will be added to your HomeMealPlanner. Then you can immediately test the planning features by generating a week plan. The meals cover different types (breakfast, lunch, dinner), cuisines, and proteins to showcase how the planning rules work.

🎯 What to Do Next

  1. Click Load Sample Meals button above
  2. Click Go to App button
  3. Go to Planner page
  4. Click Generate Week button
  5. Watch it create an optimal meal plan!
  6. Try clicking on meals to replace them
  7. Go to Rules to see planning rules in action
  8. Approve the plan to save to history
  9. Check Shopping List for ingredients

Note: Sample data is stored in your browser's localStorage. It will persist until you clear your browser data. You can export it as backup anytime.
